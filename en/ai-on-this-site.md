## The experiences are mine; content management is AI-assisted

I develop and manage this site by talking to an AI agent in natural language. I describe my own experiences, ideas and the topics I want to share, guiding the resulting content through conversation.

The agent turns those experiences into writing, edits content, prepares translations and implements technical changes to the site. I manage the content through these conversations.

## The role of AI

The experiences, views and examples of work shared here are my own. AI is my tool for expressing and publishing them, not the source of those experiences. Preparing a text with AI does not mean that AI lived or invented the experience it describes.

## Errors and feedback

This process can introduce technical inaccuracies, differences between translations or wording that drifts from what I intended. This note is not a guarantee that every piece of content has been fully verified; it explains how the content is prepared.

If you notice an issue, please [let me know](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}). Including the page link and the passage in question helps me assess it.
